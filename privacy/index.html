Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: June 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BocadoCheck collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the BocadoCheck mobile application and related services.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use BocadoCheck, we may collect:

  • Account information: email address, display name, authentication provider, and account identifiers.
  • Profile and goal information: height, weight, age, sex, activity level, calorie target, weight goal, target weight, and weekly pace.
  • Meal information: meal type, food names, portions, calories, macronutrients, fiber, nutrition quality, advisor notes, and timestamps.
  • Food photos: images you choose to capture or upload for meal analysis.
  • Weight history: weight entries you save over time.
  • Usage information: AI photo scan and manual estimate events used to operate plan limits and troubleshoot service failures.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • Authenticate your account and keep your data available across devices.
  • Save meals, weight history, goals, and daily calorie progress.
  • Estimate calories, portions, macros, fiber, and meal quality.
  • Create weekly and monthly summaries.
  • Recommend calorie targets and show progress toward goals.
  • Operate Free and Pro plan limits.
  • Protect the service, investigate errors, and respond to support requests.

AI food analysis

When you request an AI estimate, BocadoCheck may send a selected food photo or a manually entered food description to an external AI provider to identify foods and estimate portions, calories, macronutrients, fiber, and nutrition quality. A photo is not sent for analysis unless you choose to take or upload it.

AI and nutrition estimates may be inaccurate. Review and edit estimates before relying on them. BocadoCheck is not medical advice.

How information is shared

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that help operate BocadoCheck, including:

  • Supabase for authentication, database storage, file storage, and server functions.
  • OpenAI or another configured AI provider for food and nutrition analysis when you request an estimate.
  • Apple and Google if you choose to sign in using their authentication services.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business transfer subject to appropriate notice and legal protections.

Device permissions

BocadoCheck may request:

  • Camera access when you choose to take a meal photo.
  • Photo library access when you choose to upload a meal photo.
  • Notification permission when you enable meal reminders.
  • Face ID, Touch ID, or device biometrics after you opt in to app lock.

You can change these permissions in your device settings. Features that rely on a disabled permission may stop working.

Data retention and deletion

We retain account information while your account is active and as reasonably necessary to operate the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

You can delete your account from inside the app under More > Delete account. The deletion process is designed to remove your profile, meals, meal items, food photos, weight logs, AI usage events, and authentication account. You may also request help through our support page.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including authenticated access and database access controls. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

BocadoCheck is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where required.

International processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live. Those countries may have different data protection laws.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as BocadoCheck changes. We will update the date at the top of this page and provide additional notice when required by law.

Contact us

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to support@bocadocheck.com.
