BocadoCheck support

How can we help?

Tell us what happened, which device you use, and any error message you saw. Please do not email passwords, API keys, or sensitive medical information.

Email support

Common requests

Account access

Include your sign-in method and the exact error. Never send your password or verification code.

AI meal estimates

Estimates can be edited before or after saving. Send the error text if analysis fails, but only attach a food photo you are comfortable sharing.

Billing and plans

Include the platform, transaction date, and plan name. Do not send complete payment card information.

Delete your account

In the app, open More > Delete account. If you cannot sign in, email support from the address associated with the account.

Privacy questions

Review our Privacy Policy or email us with a data access or deletion request.

Response time

During the MVP period, support is handled by email. We aim to respond within three business days.
